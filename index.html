About TS Urban Allied Services

A multi-service enterprise built on disciplined execution.

TS Urban Allied Services & Enterprises (Private) Limited is a professional multi-service company committed to providing reliable and quality solutions to its clients. We cover janitorial and cleaning, security, manpower supply, freight forwarding, construction, travel, trading and more under one accountable standard.

  • Multi-sector services under single-source accountability
  • Head office in Islamabad with companies in Dubai (UAE), Saudi Arabia, the USA, the UK and Texas
  • Disciplined execution, trained personnel and transparent reporting
